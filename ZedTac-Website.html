An ERP partner built around your operations, not our software

We are an enterprise resource planning consultancy and software company, helping organizations replace disconnected tools with a single, governed system of record.

ZedTac is an enterprise resource planning consultancy and software company. We help mid-market and enterprise organizations retire disconnected spreadsheets and aging legacy tools, replacing them with a single governed system of record across finance, inventory, supply chain, manufacturing, HR and customer operations.

Our work starts with process, not licenses. We audit how your business genuinely runs, design a blueprint your leadership signs off, and implement in fixed phases under a named project lead — so digital transformation stays measurable instead of open-ended, and process automation is aimed at the bottlenecks that actually cost you money.

Everything we deliver is engineered to scale: multi-entity, multi-currency, cloud-native and API-first. And because an ERP is only worth what its adoption rate says it is, we stay engaged well beyond go-live with training, SLA-backed support and quarterly optimization reviews that keep business efficiency compounding.
